Privacy Policy

UNLARP is a live-capture social ranking app. This policy explains what information UNLARP processes, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information we process

  • Account information: Sign in with Apple identifiers and, when Apple provides them, your name or email; your UNLARP username and profile photo.
  • User content: live photos and short videos you capture in UNLARP, captions you add, comments, reactions, reports, Battle activity, and other social interactions.
  • Ranking and app activity: AI score results, leaderboard activity, streaks, feature usage, and basic diagnostics needed to operate and improve the service.
  • Region and timezone: country/state codes you choose and device timezone for regional leaderboards and daily resets. UNLARP does not require GPS location for leaderboards.
  • Notifications: device push tokens when you enable notifications.
  • Purchases: product identifiers, subscription status, and transaction information needed to fulfill and restore App Store purchases.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, score and moderate live captures, publish posts, operate leaderboards and Battles, fulfill purchases, prevent abuse, provide support, send requested notifications, and maintain service reliability.

Service providers

UNLARP uses service providers including Apple for authentication and App Store payments, Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend infrastructure, RevenueCat for purchase and subscription status, Expo for app delivery and push infrastructure, and OpenAI for visual analysis and safety moderation of submitted content.

Retention and deletion

Your UNLARP account data and uploaded content are retained while your account is active or as needed to operate the service. You can permanently delete your account from the You tab in the app. Account deletion removes your UNLARP profile, posts, social activity, and uploaded media from UNLARP systems. Apple and payment providers may retain transaction records as required for billing, fraud prevention, tax, or legal obligations.

Your choices

You can decline camera, microphone, photo-library, or notification permissions and change them later in iOS Settings. You can manually choose your leaderboard region. You can report posts and block users in the app.

Children

UNLARP is not intended for children under 13. Users who are minors where they live should use the service only with any consent required by applicable law.

Contact

For privacy, safety, account, or purchase questions, use the UNLARP Support page.
